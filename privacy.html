Spotter privacy

Spotter is a free, non-commercial training app for a small group of friends. Last updated 26 November 2026.

What is stored

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • The name you choose to show friends.
  • What you log: workouts, sets, cardio, yoga, mobility, steps, body measurements, saved links and settings.
  • If you link a wearable (such as Oura): your sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate and workout summaries from that service, plus the access token needed to fetch them.

Where it is stored

On your phone, and in a Supabase database in the United States once you sign in. Wearable access tokens are kept on the server and are never sent to the app or to other users.

Who can see it

  • You.
  • Members of groups you join see your finished workouts, at the detail level you choose for each group. Wearable health data, body measurements and private notes are not shown to groups.
  • The person who runs Spotter can access the database to keep it working.

What is not done

No ads, no selling or sharing of data with third parties, no tracking across other sites, no use of your data to train AI models.

Your choices

  • Download everything on your phone from Settings.
  • Unlink a wearable at any time in Settings. Its token is deleted and no more data is fetched. You can also revoke access from the wearable's own app.
  • Ask for your account and all its data to be deleted, using the contact below.

Contact

Ask the person who invited you, or open an issue at github.com/alphacorrections/spotter.
